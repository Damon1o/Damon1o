Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 3, 2026

The short version: this site doesn't track you. There are no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking cookies. The only personal information handled here is what you choose to send through the contact form.

Information You Provide

If you reach out through the contact form, the site collects the details you enter — your name, email address, subject, and message. That's it. Nothing is collected automatically in the background, and you can browse every page of this site without providing any personal information at all.

How Your Information Is Used

Messages sent through the contact form are used for one purpose: so I can read and respond to them. Specifically:

  • Messages are stored privately on the server where this site runs.
  • Your message may also trigger an email notification to me so I don't miss it.
  • Your information is never sold, rented, or shared with third parties.

Cookies & Local Storage

This site does not use tracking or advertising cookies. A minimal session cookie may be set for essential functionality, such as showing a confirmation message after you submit the contact form. Your light/dark theme preference is saved in your browser's local storage — it never leaves your device and doesn't identify you.

Analytics & Third Parties

No analytics or tracking services are used on this site. Pages link out to external services such as GitHub and LinkedIn — once you follow those links, their own privacy policies apply.

Data Retention & Your Rights

Contact messages are kept only as long as needed to respond and follow up. If you'd like a message you sent to be deleted, just ask — email me and I'll remove it.

Changes to This Policy

If this site's practices ever change, this page will be updated to reflect that, along with a new "last updated" date at the top.
